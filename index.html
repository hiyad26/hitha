About HITHA

Humanity starts with a helping heart.

HITHA is being designed as a Maldives-focused platform connecting people who need assistance with people who want to help.

Requests are intended to remain private during review. Approved cases can then be presented clearly, with progress and assistance updates.

Important: This website is currently a prototype. No real donations should be sent through it until the organization, payment account, verification process and legal requirements are properly established.
