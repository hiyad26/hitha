ABOUT HITHA

A place where kindness finds its way.

HITHA is being built around a simple idea: when someone needs help, finding the right person to help should not be impossible.

We aim to create a trusted, private and transparent bridge between verified needs and people willing to make a difference.

Dignity People asking for help deserve privacy and respect.
Verification Requests should be reviewed before being presented publicly.
Transparency Assistance should be traceable and responsibly recorded.
