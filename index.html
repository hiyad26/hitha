WHY HITHA?

Because everyone deserves a little help sometimes.

HITHA is built around a simple idea: people who are able to help can make a real difference to people who are struggling.

Requests are reviewed before publication, donations are recorded, and assistance is documented so that the community can see how support moves from giver to recipient.
